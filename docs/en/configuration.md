# Configuration

**Português:** [../pt-BR/configuracao.md](../pt-BR/configuracao.md)

Everything is configured on the TV in **Settings** (gear icon). Settings and keys are stored only on the TV.

![Settings](../images/settings.jpg)

## Remote control

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrows / Magic Remote pointer | Move |
| OK | Open |
| Back | Close the current screen |
| Green | Mark or unmark the focused title as watched |
| Yellow | Add or remove the focused title from My list |

## Home screen

- **Apps:** pick and reorder the apps shown (after the TV connection is accepted, every installed app is listed).
  Label each HDMI input with the device connected to it.
- **Home rows:** turn rows on or off (continue watching, live TV, weekend picks, My list, for you, releases,
  trending series, anime, kids, categories, released today, watched, news).
- **Display:** theme, clock format, language, screensaver delay and an FPS meter.

![Rows](../images/rows.jpg)

## Movies and series

- **TMDB API key:** required for releases, recommendations, search, categories and where to watch.
- **Streaming region:** defaults to the TV's region.
- **Services shown:** uncheck the services you do not use. Titles only available on unchecked services are hidden.
- **Minimum rating** and an optional **OMDb key** to use IMDb ratings instead of TMDB's.

Press OK on a title to see where to watch it, mark it as watched or add it to My list. Series also
open **Seasons & episodes** with progress, filters, order and grid or list view.

![Title details](../images/detail.jpg)
![Episodes](../images/episodes.jpg)

## Search and categories

**Search** finds movies and series by title or explores the catalog with filters for type, genre, service,
minimum rating, decade, original language and order. Category cards open the same screen with highlights.

![Explore](../images/explore.jpg)

## Kids zone

Videos from kid-safe YouTube channels and cartoons for the little ones. On by default; turn it off in Settings.

![Kids zone](../images/kids.jpg)

## IPTV (optional)

Turn on **IPTV mode** and choose the source:

- **M3U list:** the list URL.
- **Xtream Codes:** server URL, username and password.

The list is loaded when the app opens. You get **Live TV** (channels by group, ↑ ↓ to change channel),
the list's **movies and series** with its own categories, and an **IPTV** button on any title found in the
list. The built-in player resumes where you stopped and marks titles as watched near the end.

MyTVLG ships no lists or channels. Use only content you have the rights to watch.

![IPTV](../images/iptv.jpg)

## News and weather

Choose a news source (or any RSS feed). Weather uses your city or, when empty, an approximate location.

![Weather](../images/weather.jpg)
